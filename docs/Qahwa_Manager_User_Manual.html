PART I — Administrator Guide (Café Owner & Co-Owners)

OWNER MODE (PIN GATED)

1 Owner PIN Security Gate & Session Protection

All sensitive administrative actions in Qahwa Manager are protected by a cryptographic Owner PIN gate:

  • Unlocking Owner Mode: Tap the Owner Lock Icon in the top bar (or navigate to Owner Hub) and enter your 4–8 digit Owner PIN.
  • Auto-Lock & Privacy: Owner Mode automatically locks after 5 minutes of inactivity or immediately when the app goes to the background. While Owner Mode is active, Android screenshot and screen-recording protection (FLAG_SECURE) is enforced.
  • Changing the PIN: Open Owner Hub → Change Owner PIN to set a new PIN at any time.

2 Multi-Device Cloud Sync & Co-Owner Workspace Invites

Qahwa Manager works 100% offline and synchronizes automatically in real time across all your café's phones and tablets once linked to a Café Workspace:

  1. Open Owner Hub → Cloud Sync & Co-Owners and sign in or create an account with your email and password.
  2. Create Your Café Workspace: Enter your café's name (e.g., Café C Martinéz). All existing local menu items, staff, inventory, shifts, orders, spendings, and loyalty members are automatically backed up to Cloud Firestore.
  3. Invite a Co-Owner / Partner: Tap Generate Invite Code to create a single-use 8-character code (valid for 24 hours). Your co-owner signs in on their phone, taps Join with Invite Code, and immediately sees live orders, shifts, earnings, and loyalty cards.

4 Shift Settlement, Spendings & Explanatory Analytics Dashboard

  • Settling Waiter Shifts (Owner Hub → Settle Shifts): When a waiter ends their shift, enter the Counted Cash (MAD) handed over by the waiter. The app compares it against Expected Cash (Paid Orders minus Shift Spendings) and flags the shift as Exact, Short, or Over.
  • Adding Historical Earnings: In Owner Hub → Dashboard → Add Past Earnings, backfill revenue from previous days by choosing the date, Morning/Afternoon period, Waiter, and MAD amount.
  • Explanatory Owner Dashboard:
    • Gross vs. Net Earnings: Displays Total Gross Revenue, Total Spendings (daily supplies bought during shifts), and Net Earnings (Gross − Spendings).
    • Weekly Breakdown (Mon–Sun): Compares every week's revenue, shift count, daily average, week-over-week % growth badge, and highlights your Best Week.
    • Morning vs. Afternoon & Top Waiters: Explains which shift period is more lucrative per shift and shows each waiter's exact % share of revenue.

5 Setting Up the Loyalty Club, Printable Table QR Stickers & NFC Stands

Open Loyalty Cards & NFC from the Home Screen or Owner Hub:

  1. Configure Loyalty Rules (⚙️ Settings Icon in Top Bar):
    • Stamps Required (1–50): Number of drinks needed to unlock a free reward (e.g., 9).
    • Reward Title: Name of the free reward shown to customers (e.g., 1 Boisson Gratuite / قهوة مجانية).
    • Google Maps Review Link: Paste your Google Maps review URL so customers can rate your café 5 stars in one tap.
    • Guest Wi-Fi (SSID & Password): Optional Wi-Fi credentials displayed on the customer's phone when they scan a table QR code.
  2. Print Table QR Stickers (A4 / PDF):
    • Tap Print Table QR Stickers, enter the number of tables in your café (e.g., 12), and tap Print 12 Table QR Stickers (A4 / PDF).
    • Print directly to a Wi-Fi printer or save as an A4 PDF (or tap Open Web Print Studio in Browser to print from any PC at /print.html). Cut and stick each numbered QR sticker (Table 1, Table 2…) onto the matching table.
  3. Program Physical NFC Table Stands (Optional):
    • Tap Write to NFC Tag and hold the back of your Android phone against any blank NTAG213 or NTAG215 NFC sticker/card. It immediately programs the café's loyalty URL onto the chip.
  4. 15-Day Inactive Customers Win-Back ("Gbrine"):
    • Tap the Inactive 15+ days (Gbrine) filter chip to see regular customers who haven't visited in over 15 days.
    • Tap the green WhatsApp Send Icon next to any inactive customer to open WhatsApp with a pre-filled personal offer inviting them back.

PART II — Waiter & Cashier Daily Operations Guide

DAILY SHIFT & POS WORKFLOW

1 Starting a Shift, Taking Orders & Logging Shift Spendings

  • Start Shift: On the Home Screen, select your name from the active Waiter chips and tap Start Shift.
  • Take an Order (New Order): Enter the Table Number (or toggle Takeaway), tap drinks/food items to add them to the cart, and tap Place Order.
  • Record Shift Spendings: Whenever you buy daily supplies from cash during your shift (milk, mint, ice, gas), tap + Add Spending on your Shift Card, enter the reason and MAD amount, and save. It is automatically deducted from your expected shift cash.

2 Stamping Customer Loyalty Cards & Redeeming Free Drinks

Waiters can award loyalty points in two fast ways:

  • Option A — Directly at Order Payment (Recommended):
    1. Open the table's order from Open Orders.
    2. Tap Stamp Loyalty Card (+N) right above Mark as Paid (where +N equals the total drinks in the order).
    3. The camera QR scanner opens automatically — scan the customer's phone QR code (or type their phone number 06...). Their updated stamp balance appears immediately on screen, then tap Mark as Paid.
  • Option B — From the Loyalty Cards & NFC Screen:
    • Every customer who scans a Table QR sticker and registers appears automatically in your list within 1 second.
    • Tap +1 Stamp on their card (or tap Stamp by QR / Phone at the top to scan with the camera).
    • When a customer reaches the required stamps (golden card border), tap Redeem free to deduct the stamps and serve their free drink!

1 Customer Experience at the Table

  1. Scan & Choose Language: Scanning the sticker on the table opens the café's loyalty portal with a 📍 Table N badge and a FR | عربي | EN language switcher.
  2. One-Time Registration: On their first visit, the customer enters their Full Name and Phone Number (06... or 07...) and taps Activate My Card & Collect Points. Their browser remembers their card automatically for future visits, and they can tap 📲 Add My Card to Home Screen to install it like a wallet pass.
  3. Live Stamp Card & Haptic Confirmation: When the waiter stamps their card on the POS, the customer's phone vibrates and the new golden coffee cup stamp lights up live on their screen without refreshing.
  4. Digital Menu, Guest Wi-Fi & Google Reviews: From the same screen, the customer can switch to the 📖 Digital Menu tab to browse drinks & prices in their language, copy the café's Guest Wi-Fi password in one tap, or tap ⭐ Leave a 5-Star Review on Google Maps.
Tip for Returning Customers: Even if a customer switches phones or clears their browser cache, entering the same phone number (06... / 07...) on any table QR code immediately restores their existing loyalty card and all their accumulated stamps.
